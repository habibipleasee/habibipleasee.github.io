Habibi Otomasyon

Publishing tool for the Habibi Please animated series

Habibi Otomasyon is a private publishing tool used by the creator of Habibi Please, a wholesome animated comedy series about Yunus and Mariam, a married couple. The tool helps the creator prepare short videos and publish them to the creator's own accounts:

  • Instagram: @habibi.pleasee
  • YouTube: Habibi Please (@habibi.please)
  • TikTok: @habibi.please.show

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any account other than the creator's own.
