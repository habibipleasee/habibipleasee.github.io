Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how Habibi Otomasyon ("the tool") handles information. The tool is a private publishing tool operated by the creator of the Habibi Please series and is used only with the creator's own Instagram, YouTube and TikTok accounts.

1. Information the tool accesses

When the account owner connects an account, the tool receives only what the platform grants through its official API and what the owner approves on the platform's consent screen:

  • Account basics: account ID, username / display name and profile picture, used to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access tokens: credentials that let the tool publish videos on the owner's behalf.
  • Content statistics: view, like and comment counts of the owner's own posts, used to choose better posting times.

The tool does not access direct messages, contacts, followers' personal data or any account that does not belong to the owner.

2. How the information is used

  • To upload and publish videos created by the owner to the owner's own accounts.
  • To show the owner the status and performance of those videos.

The information is not sold, rented, used for advertising or shared with third parties, except that videos and their captions are sent to the platform the owner chose to publish on (Instagram, YouTube or TikTok), and video scripts are generated with an AI service (Google Gemini) that receives only the creative brief, not account data.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's personal computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by the tool. Tokens are refreshed through the platforms' official APIs and can be revoked at any time.

4. Your choices and data deletion

  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → remove "Habibi Otomasyon".
  • YouTube / Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → remove access.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions → remove the app.

After access is removed, the stored tokens stop working, and the owner deletes them from the computer. To request deletion or ask a question, email oohhabiby@gmail.com.

5. Google API Services

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed through the YouTube API Services; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Children

The tool is not directed to children and is used only by the adult account owner.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
